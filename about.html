Our Story

The Art of Giving Over

Seven years of crafting moments that live beyond the meal.

Beginnings

Born from a Decade in Tokyo

Sakura Omakase was founded in 2018 by Chef Kenji Tanaka after spending twelve formative years training under two Michelin-starred masters in Ginza and Nihonbashi. The mission was simple: to transplant the soul of Japanese omakase dining into the heart of New York City.

The name Sakura — cherry blossom — was chosen deliberately. Like the blossom, each evening is transient, extraordinarily beautiful, and never exactly repeated. It is here tonight, and tomorrow it is gone.

Our West Village counter seats exactly 12 guests per service, ensuring every person receives the chef's full attention. There are no menus. There are no choices. There is only tonight.

"Omakase is not about control. It's about surrender — and finding unexpected joy in that surrender."

— Chef Kenji Tanaka
What We Stand For

Our Four Pillars

一

Shun — Seasonality

We cook only what nature offers today. Every ingredient is sourced fresh, every dish reflects its exact moment in time.

二

Ma — Space

Between courses, between flavors, between words — we honor the Japanese concept of meaningful negative space.

三

Omotenashi — Hospitality

Our service is warm, knowledgeable, and invisibly attentive. Your comfort and delight are our only measure of success.

四

Shokunin — Mastery

Every cut, every temperature, every seasoning is an expression of years of dedicated practice. Craft over shortcuts, always.

The Experience

What is Omakase?

In Japan, omakase represents the ultimate act of culinary trust. The phrase — おまかせ — translates literally as "I leave it up to you." When you sit at the Sakura counter, you hand the reins to us.

Our 18-course tasting begins with delicate amuse-bouche and moves through five grades of nigiri, seasonal sashimi, aged fish cured in-house, a Wagyu intermezzo, and a dessert course built around Japanese sweets and seasonal fruit. Each course is plated directly before you on the hinoki wood counter.

The evening lasts approximately three hours. Sake pairing is available ($95 per person). Reservations include a non-refundable deposit equal to the prix fixe price.

The Team

Masters of Their Craft

Each member of our team has trained for a decade or more in their discipline.

Chef Kenji Tanaka

Executive Chef & Founder

Born in Osaka and trained in Ginza's most revered sushi houses, Chef Tanaka spent twelve years mastering the art of edomae sushi before founding Sakura. His approach is intuitive, seasonal, and deeply personal. He sources ingredients himself — visiting the Tsukiji outer market every Tuesday and Thursday.

Chef Yuki Mori

Pastry & Dessert Chef

Trained at the Tsuji Culinary Institute in Osaka and the Ferrandi culinary school in Paris, Chef Mori brings a Franco-Japanese sensibility to Sakura's dessert program. Her mochi variations and seasonal wagashi have become a signature closer for the omakase experience.
