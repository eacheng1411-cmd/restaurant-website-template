Our Philosophy

The Art of Omakase

Omakase — literally "I leave it up to you" — is the highest expression of trust between chef and guest. At Sakura, we honor that trust by crafting an evening that is entirely personal, seasonal, and unrepeatable.

Founded in 2018, Sakura Omakase is a 12-seat chef's table restaurant in the heart of Manhattan's West Village. Our head chef, trained at the prestigious Ginza Sushi Saito, brings 20 years of Japanese culinary tradition to every course.

"We do not cook to impress. We cook to connect."

— Chef Kenji Tanaka, Executive Chef
